# FANZi IO-nity

Welcome to the official **FANZi IO-nity** wiki.

FANZi IO-nity (`Fanzi.FanControl`) is a Windows desktop application by **Ionity Global
(Pty) Ltd** delivering AI Thermal Intelligence, Smart Fan Control, RGB Lighting, and
System Health Monitoring — powered by the AEDi (Antwerp Ecosystem Designs Ionity) AI Engine.
